# Black Clover

**Anime Adaptation:**
*A popular anime (animated television series) adaptation was created and aired from 2017 to 2021.*
  
**Characters:** 
*Asta and Yuno are the main protagonists, both orphans who were abandoned at the same church. Asta's unique ability is anti-magic, while Yuno is a powerful mage.*

**Story:**
*The story revolves around Asta and Yuno's journey to become the next Wizard King, facing various challenges and enemies.*
  
**Themes:**
*The series explores themes of perseverance, friendship, and the importance of believing in yourself, even when facing seemingly insurmountable odds.*

**Manga Publication:**
*The manga has been published in Weekly Shōnen Jump and Jump Giga.* 
  
**Anime Pause:**
*The anime adaptation paused production to avoid catching up with the manga, which is now in its final arc.*
